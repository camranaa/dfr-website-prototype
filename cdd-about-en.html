About

Establishment and legal basis

The Center for Diplomatic Development was established as a unit of the Department of Foreign Relations of the Kurdistan Regional Government on the basis of the following legal instruments:

Legal basis

  • Council of Ministers order: Order No. (3108) issued on 20/10/2021.
  • Department of Foreign Relations order: Order No. (NU26-1160) issued on 01/09/2026.

Strategic rationale

Diplomatic work in the Kurdistan Region requires highly qualified staff, alongside sound scientific research and studies that support decision-making in foreign affairs. On this basis the Center works as a strategic platform that brings two main tracks together:

  • Continuous training: building and developing the capacities of diplomatic staff to the highest standards.
  • Strategic support: producing research and analytical studies that give decision-makers insight and practical solutions.

General objective

The Center for Diplomatic Development pursues a set of comprehensive strategic objectives, foremost among them:

  • Serving society and humanityContributing actively to the causes of Kurdistan’s society and promoting shared human values.
  • Enriching knowledge and training staffBuilding human capacity in diplomacy through advanced education and training programmes.
  • Research and strategic analysisProducing sound scientific research and specialised studies in diplomacy, international relations and international studies.
  • Strengthening cultural and civilisational identityHighlighting the historical and civilisational role of the people of Kurdistan in regional and international forums.
  • Consolidating peaceful coexistenceSpreading and promoting a culture of peace, tolerance and peaceful coexistence between peoples and cultures.
