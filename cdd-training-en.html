Training

The Center runs training courses and workshops for the diplomatic staff of the Department and the KRG representations, and for related civil servants.

Programmes offered

Public diplomacy and protocol, diplomatic writing and political reporting, negotiation, digital diplomacy, international law and languages.

Who it is for

Diplomatic staff of the Department, staff of KRG representations abroad, and related ministry officials.

Methodology

Lectures, workshops and simulations, case studies, field visits, and assessment with a certificate at the end of each course.
