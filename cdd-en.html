Center for Diplomatic Development

Training diplomats, research and strategic thinking for Kurdistan Region diplomacy

The Center for Diplomatic Development is a unit of the Department of Foreign Relations dedicated to serving the society of Kurdistan and humanity, enriching knowledge and developing human capacity, and conducting scientific research in diplomacy, strategy and international studies.
